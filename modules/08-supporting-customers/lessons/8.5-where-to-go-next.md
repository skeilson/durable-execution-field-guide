# 8.5 Where to go next

**Time:** 10 minutes

You've finished the course. You can explain durable execution from first principles, you've built the same application on Restate and Temporal, and you've worked real support incidents from ticket to runbook. Here's how to keep going.

| | Restate | Temporal |

| Official docs | [docs.restate.dev](https://docs.restate.dev) | [docs.temporal.io](https://docs.temporal.io) |

Both products change quickly. When something in this course disagrees with the official docs, trust the docs, and please open an issue here so the course can be fixed.

## Ideas to practise further

- **Port a lesson to another language.** Both platforms have SDKs for Python, Go and Java. Rebuilding the shop's checkout in another language is a good test of whether you understood the concepts or just the TypeScript.
- **Write your own incident.** Pick a failure the course doesn't cover (a payment provider that rate-limits you, a workflow waiting on a webhook that never arrives) and reproduce it with the fault injection in `shared/fake-world.ts`.
- **Answer real questions.** Both communities have forums and chat where people ask the kinds of questions in this course. Answering a few is the fastest way to find the gaps in your own understanding.
- **Try a third platform.** Other durable execution tools exist, such as Inngest, DBOS and Hatchet. With the concept map from lesson 5.1, you'll recognize their ideas quickly.
